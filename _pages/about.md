Hello! I am a visiting AI research fellow at Boston University advised by [Venkatesh Saligrama](https://venkatesh-saligrama.github.io/) and [Boqing Gong](https://boqinggong.github.io/). My current research focuses on **data-efficient foundation model pre-training** inspired by **human cognitive development**. I am also interested in applications of AI in the physical and life sciences, with a focus on developing reliable automatic evaluation methods.

I graduated from BU in May 2025 with a bachelor's degree in **Physics & Computer Science**. I was very fortunate to be advised by [Boqing Gong](https://boqinggong.github.io/) and [Irving Bigio](https://www.bu.edu/eng/profile/irving-bigio/) during this time. I also spent a summer working on conversational recommendation systems as an AI/ML intern at Adobe and I continue to collaborate closely with Adobe Research. I am best reached by email at `ac25@bu.edu`.
